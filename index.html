Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

ChoreFun ("the App") is developed by Code Splitters. A parent sets tasks, a child marks them done and earns Coins, and the family saves them towards things they have chosen. This policy explains what the App keeps, where it keeps it, and how to get rid of it.

The short version

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing sold or shared with anybody. A parent needs an account, because a family's tasks have to reach another phone. A child needs no account at all.

What the App collects

  • The parent's name and email address, given by Apple when you sign in with Apple - a private relay address instead of your own, if you choose to hide it. The name is what the family is called after. The email address sits in the sign-in record; the App neither shows it nor uses it, and we send no email to it.
  • An account identifier from Apple. It is what a family and a phone hang on.
  • What the family writes: each child's first name and emoji, task titles, Pot names, reward prices, and the record of what was done, approved and paid out.
  • A child's device records itself in the family when it joins with a code, so a parent can see it and disconnect it.

Nothing else. No contacts, no photos, no location, no advertising identifier, no health data, no microphone or camera.

Where it is kept

On the phones in your family, and with Google Firebase - Authentication and Cloud Firestore - which stores it on our behalf so that a parent's phone and a child's phone see the same list. Google processes it for us and for nothing of its own. Security rules limit every document to the family it belongs to.

A few things stay on the phone itself and never leave it: which role that phone has, the colours each mode wears, and how the Pots are shown.

Children's privacy

The App is made for families, and the children using it are about 9 to 12. A child's information is entered by their parent, and it is deliberately as little as the App can work with: a first name or a nickname, and an emoji. Use a nickname if you would rather - the App does not mind.

A child is never asked for an email address, never asked to create an account, and never signs in with anything of their own: their phone joins the family with a code and is signed in anonymously. There is no chat in the App, no profile anybody outside the family can see, no way for anyone outside the family to contact a child through it, and no advertising of any kind.

A parent can remove a child, disconnect a child's phone, or delete the whole family, at any time, in the App.

No tracking, no advertising, no analytics

The App contains no advertising framework, no analytics tool and no tracking software. The only third party behind it is Google Firebase, above. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared for anyone else's purposes.

Deleting your data

In the App: Settings → My account → Delete account. It deletes your account, and if you are the last parent in the family it takes the family with it - the children, the tasks, the Coins, the Pots and the whole record. Removing a child removes that child's data. Deleting the App from a phone takes the App's own files with it, but iOS keeps the record that the phone was signed in with your Apple Account - so reinstalling may find you already signed in. Signing out, or deleting your account, is what ends that.

If something cannot be deleted from inside the App, email us and we will do it.

Your rights

The App is first released in the United Kingdom. Under UK data protection law you may ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you think we have handled it badly. The quickest route to all of it is the email address below.
