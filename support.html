ChoreFun

An iPhone app for a family's tasks. A parent sets the tasks and what each one is worth, a child marks them done, and the Coins land once a parent has had a look. What the Coins are worth is up to the family; no money moves in the App.

Getting help

Email chorefun@codesplitters.com with any question, problem or suggestion. If something looks wrong on a screen, a screenshot helps more than anything else.

Common questions

How does my child get the app on their phone?
Install the App on their phone, and choose a kid when it asks who is using the phone. On your own phone, open your child's profile and show them the code it gives you. The code lasts a day; make a new one whenever you need it.

Can both parents use it?
Yes. Settings → Invite a parent, or the same row on your family's screen, gives you a code for them. You both see the same children, the same tasks and the same Coins.

Why do Coins not arrive straight away?
A task a child marks done waits for a parent to approve it. The Coins land then. It is one tap, and it is deliberate: it keeps the two of you talking about what was actually done.

What is a Pot?
Somewhere to save Coins towards one thing - a football, a day out. A child can put Coins in a Pot and watch it fill. You hand the real thing over when it is full.

How do I take a child out, or a phone?
In your child's profile. You can disconnect a phone that joined with a code, and you can remove a child.

How do I delete everything?
Settings → My account → Delete account. If you are the last parent in the family it deletes the family too - the children, tasks, Coins, Pots and the record.

Privacy

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, and a child needs no account. See the privacy policy.
